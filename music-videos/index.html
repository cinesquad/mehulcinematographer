What We Bring

From pre-production scouting to the final colour grade, Mehul handles the entire cinematography pipeline for music video productions.

Cinematic Framing

Every shot composed with intentional framing — matching the emotional arc and cadence of the music.

Lighting Design

Dramatic, mood-matching lighting setups built to suit the song's energy — from intimate folk to high-energy anthems.

Sync & Rhythm

Dynamic motion and gimbal choreography that breathes with the track — beat-matched pacing and sync-conscious framing.

Location Scouting

From the sun-baked salt pans of Kutch to historic havelis in Ahmedabad — discovering frames with visual character.

Colour Science & Finish

Optical film emulation and tailored colour grading giving every music video a distinct, screen-ready personality.
