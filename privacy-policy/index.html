Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Data Controller: Mehul Prajapati

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Mehul Prajapati (independent filmmaker & photographer in Ahmedabad, operating at mehulcinematographer.com) collects, uses, and safeguards information submitted through this website.

The data controller for this website is Mehul Prajapati, based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. If you have any inquiries regarding your privacy or personal information, contact directly at contact@mehulcinematographer.com or via phone/WhatsApp at +91 9429 225 222.

2. Information Collected

When you interact with this portfolio website, we collect only information necessary to respond to production briefs and booking enquiries:

  • Enquiry Data: When submitting the contact form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, agency or brand name, shoot dates, location, and creative project briefs.
  • Direct Communication: If you initiate a conversation via WhatsApp or phone, your message history and contact details are maintained strictly for production planning and correspondence.
  • Analytics & Usage Telemetry: Anonymized website performance data is collected via Google Analytics 4 (e.g., page views, approximate geographic region, device type, bounce rate) to optimize loading speed and accessibility. No personally identifying information is shared or sold.

3. How Information Is Used

Information provided through this website is used solely to:

  • Review commercial, corporate, musical, or narrative project briefs.
  • Provide technical estimates, crew recommendations, and production timelines.
  • Coordinate shoot logistics, gear packages, and call sheets upon project confirmation.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, advertising brokers, or telemarketers.

4. Embedded Media & Third-Party Services

Pages on this site feature video players embedded from YouTube. Embedded videos behave in the exact same manner as if the visitor has visited YouTube directly. YouTube may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with embedded content if you are logged into that service.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

Enquiry emails and messages are retained only as long as necessary for ongoing production discussions or project archives. You have the right to request a copy of any personal details you have provided, or request that your contact data be permanently deleted from our records by contacting contact@mehulcinematographer.com.
