The Alchemy of Jewellery Lighting

Jewellery commercial cinematography is one of the most exacting optical disciplines in filmmaking. Unlike standard commercials, fine jewellery consists of polished precious metals, faceted gemstones, and intricate craftsmanship that immediately expose uncalibrated lighting.

Working with trusted jewelers such as Patel Jewellers, Mahakali Jewellers, and Pachichighar, Mehul designs bespoke cross-polarized illumination schemes that bring out diamond dispersion, accentuate 22K gold warmth, and illuminate intricate polki jadau details while flattering bridal skin tones.

Optical & On-Set Execution

  • Dedicated Cine Macro: 1:1 reproduction optics capturing micro-filigree and gemstone clarity.
  • Motorized Sliders: Smooth fluid camera glides for dynamic light play across faceted stones.
  • High-Speed 4K: 60fps to 120fps recording for slow-motion drapery, veil reveals, and bridal radiance.
  • Color Grading: Accurate DaVinci Resolve color pipelines preserving genuine gold tonality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you light reflective gold and diamonds without blowing out highlights?

Jewellery cinematography requires specialized cross-polarized light control, large diffusion flags, and micro-grid modifiers. We sculpt reflections to reveal gold purity and diamond fire while preserving natural skin tones on models.

Do you bring specialized macro lenses and high-speed camera rigs?

Yes. Every jewellery shoot uses dedicated cinema macro optics (1:1 magnification), motorized slider tracks for parallax movement, and high frame rate capabilities (up to 120fps) for fluid slow-motion sparkle.

Can you travel for multi-day jewellery campaigns outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Mehul frequently travels across Gujarat (Surat, Rajkot, Vadodara) and pan-India (Mumbai, Jaipur, Delhi) with complete travel-ready camera packages and lighting coordination.
