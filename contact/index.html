Production Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding shoot schedules, equipment packages, and travel availability.

How far in advance should we book for commercial shoots?

For national television commercials and large corporate documentaries, 2 to 4 weeks advance notice is ideal to conduct storyboard breakdowns, recce / location scouting, and lighting design. Urgent productions can also be accommodated based on schedule availability.

Are you available for shoots outside Gujarat and internationally?

Yes. Mehul travels frequently for projects across India (regularly filming in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad) as well as overseas (including Dubai and the UAE). Complete travel and gear logistics are handled efficiently.

What camera and optical packages are provided?

We supply turnkey camera packages including the Sony FX3 Cinema Line (Full-Frame 4K 120fps), Sony G Master Cine primes and zooms, Cooke anamorphic optics, DJI Ronin gimbal systems, and wireless director monitoring setups.

Do you assist with post-production color grading?

Yes. Every project captured in S-Log3 can be supervised through DaVinci Resolve Studio with ACES color management, custom film-emulation grain LUTs, and mastering for broadcast TV and digital platforms.
