The Journey & Visual Philosophy

With over 13 years of experience behind the lens, I have crafted visual stories across a wide spectrum of industries — from high-stakes corporate and industrial films to commercial advertising, music videos, tourism campaigns, and experimental cinema.

Working as an independent cinematographer, a long-term retainer, and leading entire project production management under 9th Sky Network LLP, I deliver high-impact visuals for brands, agencies, and artists who demand uncompromising excellence.

Cinematography is an alchemy of optical engineering and emotional instinct. Whether executing the high-velocity precision required for luxury automotive campaigns (such as BMW Gallops Autohaus), the immense architectural scale of heavy manufacturing facilities (Plasser India, Gokul Agro, GMDC), or the intimate lyrical rhythm of hit music videos, every composition is crafted with deliberate intent and visual gravity.
